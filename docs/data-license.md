# Data and licensing policy

## Code

The application code is released under the MIT License.

## Archive data

The canonical archive is a curated compilation. The project does not automatically grant a blanket copyright licence to material that originated elsewhere.

Until a dataset-specific open-data licence is formally reviewed and applied to a release, treat archive records as a catalogue/compilation whose individual source material may carry separate rights.

A future data release may use a dedicated licence for original structured contributions, but that licence must not be interpreted as licensing third-party texts, quotations, images, recordings or source publications.

## Third-party material

Always follow the rights and licence terms of the cited source or media provider. The repository's MIT licence applies to code, not to third-party cultural material.

## Quotations

Quoted material is included for research/reference purposes and remains attributed to its source. Users should consult the cited edition or provider for the authoritative text and applicable rights.

## Corrections

If a record appears to make a rights claim that the evidence does not support, report it as a correction rather than assuming the archive owns the material.
