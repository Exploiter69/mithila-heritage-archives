Phases 0–32 established the canonical archive, provenance model, relations, research infrastructure, media handling, atlas, graph, literature/language surfaces, editorial workflow, deterministic audits and deployment hardening.

Phase 33 established the scholarly verification readiness workflow. It intentionally does not replace human source verification.

## Release policy

Release tags describe the software/data snapshot in Git. They do not imply that every cultural claim is permanently verified. Individual records retain their own evidence and editorial status.
